LOCAL SETUP

Setup & privacy

Connect your own Spotify developer app.

Spotify connection

Saved only in this browser. Use your Client ID, never your client secret. Disconnect Spotify before changing it.

  1. In Spotify Developer Dashboard, register this redirect URI: http://127.0.0.1:4173/
  2. Paste your Client ID above and choose Save Client ID.
  3. Choose Connect Spotify.

Use a Client ID only. PKCE sign-in does not use a client secret.

Spotify development apps may restrict which accounts and playlists are accessible. The app reports failed reads and incomplete scans.

Your data

Access and refresh tokens live in this browser tab’s session storage. Scans and choices live in memory and disappear on reload. Export only when you want a local copy. Disconnect clears the session and scan. Your saved Client ID stays in this browser until you choose Forget saved ID. Song preferences are saved in local storage per Spotify account, and can be removed on each song card. They apply to local review choices only.

Requested permissions: read private playlists and include collaborative playlists. There are no Spotify playlist write requests in this app.

Matches use metadata rules. No Spotify content is used to train a machine-learning model.
